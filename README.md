# Privacy Policy – Original Accessories AI

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

## 1. About Us

Original Accessories AI is an application developed to support the operations and customer service of our own Instagram store, @original.accessories.am, based in Armenia.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interactions with our store, we may collect:

- Instagram usernames and account identifiers.
- Messages, comments, images and voice messages.
- Names, phone numbers and delivery addresses provided by customers.
- Order, reservation and payment confirmation information.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Respond to customer inquiries.
- Provide product information and availability.
- Process orders and reservations.
- Arrange deliveries.
- Provide customer support.
- Improve our customer service.

Our application may use artificial intelligence to assist with customer communications. Human review is used where necessary.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We may use Meta APIs, OpenAI, Telegram and secure hosting services to support our store operations.

Information is shared with service providers only as necessary to provide these services. We do not sell customer personal information.

## 5. Data Retention

Our standard retention periods are:

- Messages and transcripts: 6 months.
- Customer images and payment confirmation screenshots: 30 days.
- Voice recordings: 7 days.
- Customer profiles and order history: 1 year after the last interaction or order.

Information may be retained longer where required by applicable law.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and loss.

## 7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

We will handle these requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal obligations change.
